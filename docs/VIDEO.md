# Helpa: the launch video

**Length:** 2:57. **Format:** 1920x1080. **Built in:** `media/edit.py` from `media/timeline.json`.

**What a judge should remember:** Helpa reads *my exact machine*, shows me the fix on a 3D twin of it, checks my work, and when it's beyond me, gets the right pro or part at a fair price. Five Meta models, live, built in this hackathon.

The cut alternates three kinds of shot:

- **Split:** you on the left (talking to camera, or your hands on the real machine), the live app in a phone frame on the right.
- **Full screen:** your footage, or an animated diagram explaining what's happening underneath.
- **The app is never staged.** Every phone screen is the live app at helpa.pyras.org making real Meta calls with photos of your machines: Frigidaire dryer FFRE4120SW2, Frigidaire washer FFTW4120SW2, Samsung microwave ME16A4021AS, Samsung range, a 2015 Camry.

### Setup (do this once)

- **Camera:** iPhone back camera, **landscape**, 4K 30 fps (Settings → Camera → Record Video). Don't use the selfie camera.
- **Tripod or a stack of books** at chest height, about 1.2 m (4 ft) away, lens at eye level.
- **Stand in the center of the frame.** Your clip is cropped into its own panel on the left of the video and the app sits beside it, never on top of you. The crop keeps the middle 60% of your frame, so anything near the edges gets cut. Head and shoulders, with a little space above your head.
- **Background:** the laundry room with the dryer behind you, lights on. If it's too tight, stand in front of the kitchen with the stove in soft focus. No window behind you.
- **Light:** face a window or the brightest light. Turn off any light that's behind you.
- **Sound:** this decides whether the video feels cheap. Put a second iPhone in your shirt pocket, mic up, recording Voice Memos, and **clap once on camera at the start of every take** so I can sync it. No AirPods: Bluetooth makes voices thin. Turn off the dryer, fans and the TV.
- **Look:** a plain solid T-shirt (no logos, no stripes).

### A: talking to camera (7 files)

- **Talk to a person, not a lens.** Tape a small photo of a friend right beside the lens and explain Helpa to them, like you're showing them something that blew your mind.
- **Know the line, don't read it.** Say it out loud five times before you hit record. If a word trips you up, swap it for your own words; the meaning matters, not the exact script.
- **Move a little.** Lean on the washer, gesture toward the dryer, hold the towel. Standing frozen reads as nervous.
- **Smile through the pain points** ("a hundred and fifty bucks just to show up"). It signals you already solved it.
- **Energy one notch above normal.** On camera, normal reads as tired.
- Do each line **3 times** in one recording, with a 2-second pause between takes. I'll pick the best and cut the pauses.

### B: hands and machine (10 files, mostly on your Ray-Ban Meta glasses)

**Shoot these POV on the glasses.** The viewer sees exactly what you see: your hands, your phone with Helpa on it, the real machine. You don't need a friend, it looks native and modern, and it's Meta hardware in a Meta hackathon video. Only `B0` needs the iPhone on the tripod, because it has to show your face.

**Glasses settings and technique:**
- In the Meta AI app, set video to the highest quality it offers, and clean the lens before every shot.
- Start each clip with the capture button, **count "one, two" in your head**, do the action, hold the end pose for 2 seconds, then stop. I need that handle on both ends.
- **Move your head like a steadicam:** slowly, and only when you have to. Point your face at your hands, not your eyes. The camera sits on the left temple, so keep the action slightly right of where you'd normally look.
- **Keep the phone screen readable:** hold it at chest height, about 30 cm (1 ft) from your face, tilted toward you, brightness all the way up. Avoid glare from ceiling lights.
- Good light on the machine: laundry room lights on, plus a lamp if you have one.
- The glasses' mics are great for your voice, so `B5` is recorded on them too.
- Export the clips from the Meta AI app to your camera roll, then AirDrop them.

### Music

Pick one track: upbeat and warm, around 100–120 BPM, no vocals, with a clear lift you can feel. ## How it's built

- `media/rec.py` drives the live app in a phone-sized browser and records clips at 780x1688.
  - `CAMERA=` feeds a real video into the camera, so the viewfinder shows the real machine.
  - `MIC=` feeds a voice into the mic, so Say it is real speech transcribed by Muse Voice.
- `media/takes.sh` holds the final takes.
- `media/diagrams/` holds the animated scenes; `render.py` records them.
- `media/edit.py` renders `timeline.json` to `media/out/helpa-demo.mp4`:
  - picks up `footage/` files by slot name
  - crossfades every cut
  - normalizes your voice to -16 LUFS
  - ducks the music under it
