# Helpa: 3-minute video plan

One story, done deep: a dryer that leaves clothes wet, fixed for real with Helpa watching. Then the car on its exact
model, a fast montage, the proof, the Meta models. Real machines, real hands, phone screen recordings cut in.

The line to land: **a chatbot tells you what to do. Helpa shows you on your machine and watches you do it.**

## Voiceover script

> Two hours in the dryer. Still wet. And the car's overdue for an oil change.
>
> So you ask a chatbot. You get six hundred words, and none of them show you which part is the lint screen.
>
> This is Helpa. Snap the sticker, and Helpa reads your exact model. Tell it what's wrong. Agents research the
> manual, build the 3D model and plan the fix, all at once.
>
> Good news: you can fix this yourself. Here's why. Hot air gets stuck at a clogged lint screen, so your clothes
> never dry.
>
> Now point at your dryer. Helpa shows the move on your machine. Your turn. Helpa watches, and knows when you've got
> it. Clean it, slide it back. Then a real test. Strong warm air at the vent: fixed. It remembers your dryer for next
> time.
>
> Same thing for the car, on its exact engine. Fourteen millimeter drain plug, oil into the pan. Four point four
> quarts of 0W-20, then check the dipstick.
>
> Try a sample with nothing broken. Any other machine gets a model built on the spot. And you can ask about it later.
>
> We ran fifty real problems. Every dangerous one went to a pro. Same steps as a chatbot, three times less to read,
> and you can see where the part is.
>
> Built on Meta's models: Wonder researches, Spark plans and checks, SAM finds your parts live.
>
> Helpa. It understands your machine and guides your hands.

## Recording tips

- **Android (Seeker):** Quick Settings → Screen record → "Record audio: device audio and microphone" for voice input
  shots, or "None" to keep it clean. Turn on "Show touches" (Developer options) for tap shots. Brightness to max,
  Do Not Disturb on.
- **iPhone:** Control Center → Screen Recording; long-press it to turn the microphone on. Settings → Display &
  Brightness, Auto-Lock off while filming. Focus mode on so notifications don't show.
- Film the phone over the real machine with a second phone or camera for the AR shots (10 to 13, 17); the screen
  recording alone doesn't show the room. Lock exposure on the filming camera so the phone screen doesn't blow out.
- Light the laundry room evenly; no backlight from a window behind the dryer. Wipe the phone lens.
- Run a "Try a sample" pass first to warm everything up; record the real run second.
- Captions on every line (people watch muted on X). Label each Meta model on screen when it works (SAM 3.1 on the
  lock, Spark on the check).
