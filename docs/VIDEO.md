# Helpa: the launch video

**Length:** 2:57. **Format:** 1920x1080. **Built in:** `media/edit.py` from `media/timeline.json`.

**What a judge should remember:** Helpa reads *my exact machine*, shows me the fix on a 3D twin of it, checks my work, and when it's beyond me, gets the right pro or part at a fair price. Five Meta models, live, built in this hackathon.

The cut alternates three kinds of shot:

- **Split:** you on the left (talking to camera, or your hands on the real machine), the live app in a phone frame on the right.
- **Full screen:** your footage, or an animated diagram explaining what's happening underneath.
- **The app is never staged.** Every phone screen is the live app at helpa.pyras.org making real Meta calls with photos of your machines: Frigidaire dryer FFRE4120SW2, Frigidaire washer FFTW4120SW2, Samsung microwave ME16A4021AS, Samsung range, a 2015 Camry.

## What you record

### Setup (do this once)

- **Camera:** iPhone back camera, **landscape**, 4K 30 fps (Settings → Camera → Record Video). Don't use the selfie camera.
- **Tripod or a stack of books** at chest height, about 1.2 m (4 ft) away, lens at eye level.
- **Stand in the center of the frame.** The edit keeps the middle 60% of the picture, so anything near the edges gets cut. Head and shoulders, with a little space above your head.
- **Background:** the laundry room with the dryer behind you, lights on. If it's too tight, stand in front of the kitchen with the stove in soft focus. No window behind you.
- **Light:** face a window or the brightest light. Turn off any light that's behind you.
- **Sound:** this decides whether the video feels cheap. Use wired earbuds with a mic, or AirPods, recording into the camera phone. Or record a second iPhone's Voice Memos in your shirt pocket and clap once at the start of each take so I can sync it. Kill the dryer, fans and the TV.
- **Look:** a plain solid T-shirt (no logos, no stripes).

Do each line **3 times** in one recording, with a 2-second pause between takes. I'll pick the best and cut the pauses. Smile on the first word. Small hand gestures are good.

### Music

Pick one track: upbeat and warm, around 100–120 BPM, no vocals, with a clear lift you can feel. Use YouTube Audio Library (Studio → Audio Library) or Uppbeat (free tier, gives you a license code to paste in the post). Don't use a popular song: X and the hackathon platform can mute it. Save it as `media/footage/music.mp3`; the edit keeps it low and ducks it under your voice automatically.

## How it's built

- `media/rec.py` drives the live app in a phone-sized browser and records clips at 780x1688.
  - `CAMERA=` feeds a real video into the camera, so the viewfinder shows the real machine.
  - `MIC=` feeds a voice into the mic, so Say it is real speech transcribed by Muse Voice.
- `media/takes.sh` holds the final takes.
- `media/diagrams/` holds the animated scenes; `render.py` records them.
- `media/edit.py` renders `timeline.json` to `media/out/helpa-demo.mp4`:
  - picks up `footage/` files by slot name
  - crossfades every cut
  - normalizes your voice to -16 LUFS
  - ducks the music under it
