## Part 1: you on camera (3 clips, iPhone)

**Setup:**
- iPhone back camera, held sideways, on a tripod or a stack of books at chest height, about 4 feet away.
- You in the middle of the frame, chest up, dryer behind you.
- Lights on, no window behind you, plain shirt.

**Audio:**
- Put a second phone in your shirt pocket recording Voice Memos.
- Clap once on camera at the start of each clip so I can sync it.
- Turn off fans and the TV.

**How:**
- Do each clip 3 times in the same recording.
- Talk to the camera like it's a friend, with a little more energy than normal.

**Every clip:**
- Start recording, wait 2 seconds, do the action slowly, hold still 2 seconds, stop.
- Move your head slowly and keep your hands a little to the right.

**Audio:** silent except `G7` (I mute the rest).

## Part 3: voiceover (one Voice Memo, lines V1 to V18)

**Where and how:**
- A closet or a small quiet room.
- Phone 6 inches from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- Read each line twice, with a 2-second pause after.
- Warm and a little amazed, like showing a friend. Messed up? Pause and redo it.

The lines are the `V` rows in the script above, in order.
