## Part 2: voiceover (one Voice Memo)

**Where:** a closet full of clothes, or a small room with the door shut. No fans, no AC.

**How:**
- Hold the phone 6 inches from your mouth, slightly off to the side so your breath doesn't hit it.
- Read each line **twice**, with a 2-second pause after each.
- Sound like you're showing a friend something that blew your mind: warm, a little amazed. Not a newscaster, not a hype guy.
- Smile while you say the money lines (#2). You can hear it.
- Mess up? Pause and say the line again. I'll cut it.

1. "My clothes came out of the dryer soaking wet, and I've never fixed a dryer in my life."
2. "A repair visit is a hundred and fifty bucks just to show up. A chatbot gives me twelve paragraphs. So I built Helpa."
3. "I point it at the sticker and say what's wrong."
4. "Muse Spark reads my exact model, and asks the one question that changes the fix."
5. "Five Meta models fire at once. Muse Wonder searches the web for my manual and the cause. Spark builds a 3D twin. SAM 3.1 finds the parts on my photo. And Muse Image draws the fix right on it."
6. "It tells me what's likely wrong in one line, then gives me the plan."
7. "That's my actual dryer, redrawn with the fix on it."
8. "Every step plays on a 3D twin of my exact machine. On a Frigidaire the lint screen is in the door, not on top, so that's where it shows me."
9. "It's not a video. The sticker picks a model with real joints, and Muse Spark maps every step onto them. Something it's never seen? It builds the model on the spot."
10. "Not sure which way it comes out? I just ask."
11. "Then I snap what I did. First try, it tells me I'm not done. It actually checks."
12. "Still damp? It rules out that cause by name and goes after the next one. And when it's dangerous, it stops me."
13. "When it's beyond me, Helpa works as my agent. It finds the best-reviewed shops near me and what this should cost."
14. "It writes the message with my model and what I already tried."
15. "And with my permission, it checks the quote and books it."
16. "And the job updates. The part, the price, fair or not."
17. "And it's not a dryer app. My washer."
18. "My microwave: it figured out it's stuck in demo mode."
19. "My stove."
20. "Even my car."
21. "It finds the exact part, tracks it, and when it shows up, walks me through putting it in."
22. "These are agents doing the boring parts of owning a home."
23. "Everything you just saw is live, built on Meta's models during this hackathon, and it's open source."
24. "Helpa. Fix what's broken, even if you know nothing. Try it at helpa dot pyras dot org."
