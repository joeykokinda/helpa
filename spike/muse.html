Meet Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full

←→to navigate
Coding at the frontier
Solve complex engineering tasks in large repositories, making and verifying changes across multiple files in parallel.
Long-running agents
Hand off multi-step tasks that run for hours, uninterrupted. Muse Wonder plans the work, delegates parts to sub-agents, calls tools in parallel and recovers from errors to keep your critical work on-track.
Advanced computer use
Power agents that see and operate browsers, desktop, and mobile apps to complete everyday tasks.
Active memory
Carries important context through conversations and active focus keeps Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full working towards your goals, even when a task gets challenging.
Muse Wonder Full
Muse Wonder Full is our most advanced model yet and tops agentic and coding benchmarks, including DeepSWE and SWE-Marathon in independent third-party runs.
